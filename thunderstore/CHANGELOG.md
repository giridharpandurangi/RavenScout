# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Raven Roost: send a raven on long scouting trips in any direction, in real in-game time.
- Map reveal along the route and pins for dungeons, boss altars, traders, villages and other places,
  anywhere in the world; ore and optional wild food in land someone has been near.
- Live tracking: a feather on the map and minimap, the map revealed as it flies, pins as it passes.
- Food makes the raven fly farther and faster; a hungry raven flies short and slow.
- Eight roost tiers from Wooden to Deep North: more range, speed and sight; ore and food spotting unlock.
- Scout Reports written from the roost's point of view, with Vague, Rough or Exact distances; Copy for
  friends; the feather guide.
- No-map worlds detected automatically: no map changes, a Scout Report instead (NoMapMode to choose).
- Multiplayer: the server tracks trips; findings private or shared with everyone, with catch-up for
  players who join later. Gameplay settings synced from the server.
